## Needs a commercial licence

Anything done in or for a business, whatever its size. For example:

- Using it inside a company, including internally on company vehicles
- Shipping it as part of a product or an appliance
- Running it as part of a paid or ad supported service
- Using it in consulting or integration work you are paid for
- Bundling it with hardware you sell
- Fleet management, leasing, rental or workshop operations

If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask before you deploy.

## How to ask

Open an issue in this repository titled `Commercial licence request` at
<https://github.com/boesec/vag2mqtt/issues>. Please describe:

1. Who you are: company, size, country
2. What you want to do with VAG2MQTT
3. Roughly how many vehicles or installations are involved
4. Whether you intend to redistribute it or run it yourself

Terms are agreed individually and typically involve a share of the revenue the
software helps generate. There is no public price list.
